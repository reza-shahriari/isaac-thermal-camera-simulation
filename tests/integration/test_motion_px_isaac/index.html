Motion px isaac

In-sim synthesised motion: the roadmap M10.1b acceptance criteria, against the renderer.

tests/integration/test_motion_px_isaac.py on GitHub

tests/unit/test_motion_px.py holds the arithmetic to exact values on transforms it makes up. What it cannot check is the half that talks to USD: that the transforms read off a stage are the ones the renderer drew with, in the convention the projection expects, and broadcast by an instance id that means the prim it appears to mean. Those are four separate chances to be plausibly wrong, and all of them produce smooth, believable motion of the wrong magnitude or sign.

So this renders a real prim at a known displacement and asks what came back. The bar is the roadmap's: a bar moving at 3 px/frame reports 3.0 within a tenth of a pixel, a static scene reports 0, and a prim moving with the camera reports 0.
