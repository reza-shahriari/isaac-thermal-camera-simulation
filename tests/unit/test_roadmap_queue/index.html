Roadmap queue

The roadmap's "do next" queue resolves to exactly one step, deterministically.

tests/unit/test_roadmap_queue.py on GitHub

The roadmap's stated picking rule -- "the first step in phase order whose deps are all ticked" -- does not pick a step. On revision 4 the day it landed, 51 of 109 open steps satisfied it at once, 13 of them in phase 0. What was actually choosing was the row's position in the table, which is not a rule: it changes when anyone reorders a table, and it cannot be argued with.

scripts/next_step.py replaces that with a topological sort over a documented total order. These tests are what make it safe to type "do next" and act on the answer without reading the document:

  • the order is total -- one head, no ties, and two runs agree;
  • it is sound -- no step is ever emitted before something it depends on;
  • it is complete -- every open step appears exactly once, so nothing is silently dropped;
  • the promotions are real ids with real reasons, and there are few of them.
