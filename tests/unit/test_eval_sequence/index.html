Eval sequence

The sequence reader and the static-clip gate (ME.1b).

tests/unit/test_eval_sequence.py on GitHub

Two things are under test and they fail in different ways. The reader fails loudly -- a missing frame or a widened dtype raises. The classifier fails silently, and that is why most of this file is about it: a panned clip that gets called static produces a per-pixel temporal standard deviation that is larger than the sensor's, perfectly smooth, and indistinguishable from a noisier camera. The two controls the roadmap names -- a translated clip and a jittered one -- are the whole point, so they are built with the same per-frame step size and must come out opposite.

docs/physics-model.md §10.2, §15 T4; ADR 0003.
