Occlusion bound

OC.8 -- what partial occlusion actually costs, in kelvin (ADR 0129, ADR 0131).

tests/unit/test_occlusion_bound.py on GitHub

The plan said the error was "bounded". This is the bound. Three fills are compared against a two-layer reference on the same scene at three depth ratios:

  • OC.6 alone -- normalise against the layers that are there;
  • OC.8 -- push-pull the farthest layer into the gap;
  • OC.7 -- hand the stage the true background, which only sky and sea can do.

Reported in apparent temperature, because that is the unit the error matters in: a fringe of a few tenths of a kelvin is under a Boson's NETD and a fringe of several kelvin is a feature a detector will learn.
