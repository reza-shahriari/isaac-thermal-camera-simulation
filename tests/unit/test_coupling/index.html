Coupling

TC.3 — contactors and radiation between fields.

tests/unit/test_coupling.py on GitHub

Two overlapping patches at h_c = 1e4 W m⁻² K⁻¹ must give a total conductance h_c · A_overlap and the same total after one grid is refined four times -- the property a conductance authored per node cannot have, and the reason a contactor is defined by overlap area. The coupled solve is one solver, so a joint whose time constant is a fraction of a second stands under a 60 s tick and the stored energy of two patches joined by a contactor is conserved to solver precision.

Radiation reuses ADR 0088's view factors in both directions: the power an underbody radiates onto the road equals the power the road's cells receive, and reciprocity is checked against an independent quadrature from the body's side.

docs/physics-model.md §6.1, §6.4; ADR 0087, ADR 0088, ADR 0094, ADR 0099; roadmap TC.3.
