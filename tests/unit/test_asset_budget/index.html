Asset budget

AI.3 -- what an imported asset costs, and how much of that cost buys anything.

tests/unit/test_asset_budget.py on GitHub

Two separate claims are tested here and they fail for different reasons.

Affordability is a measurement someone else made: GT.7 timed 102,400 cells over a 48-hour spin-up at 11.5 s, against Fraunhofer's 1,313,410-triangle reference. If the budget drifts away from that, the number stops meaning anything.

Usefulness is physics. A cell holds one temperature, and two cells closer together than sqrt(alpha dt) cannot hold different ones, because conduction erases the difference inside the step. A test that only counted triangles would pass on a mesh with a million faces on a postage stamp, which is the actual condition of the only imported asset this project has.
