Layered defocus

OC.6 -- depth-varying defocus by layered compositing (ADR 0129).

tests/unit/test_layered_defocus.py on GitHub

The headline test is test_the_background_stays_sharp_behind_a_blurred_foreground: one kernel for the frame blurs a sharp background because the foreground was out of focus, and that is the defect layering exists to remove. Everything else here guards a property that makes the composite trustworthy -- ordering, sky, and the flat field.
