Gbuffer isaac

Engine-free tests for the Isaac geometry adapter (roadmap M10.1).

tests/unit/test_gbuffer_isaac.py on GitHub

The adapter's arithmetic -- per-pixel ray directions, the two cosines, V_s, the motion scaling, the sky mask -- needs no renderer, so it is verified here in milliseconds against closed forms rather than inside a 35 s Kit boot. tests/integration/test_gbuffer_isaac.py then checks only what genuinely needs the engine: that the AOVs exist, carry float32, and agree with the scene.

The sphere case is the one that catches the expensive bug. Taking the cosine against the optical axis (or reading a view-space normal's z channel) is right at the principal point and wrong everywhere else; test_sphere_cos_theta_needs_the_per_pixel_ray asserts both that the adapter matches the closed form and that the axis shortcut would miss by more than the tolerance.
