Raycast

PT.22 — shadows from geometry, neighbours included, and the sun's finite disc.

tests/unit/test_raycast.py on GitHub

Three claims. The mesh path and the rectangle path agree cell-for-cell on a box occluder, so swapping the representation cannot change the physics. A neighbouring block shades a wall that the wall's own mesh never can, which is what "the query walks every opaque prim" means. And the 0.53 deg solar disc, sampled on 7 to 37 rays, makes the terminator a ramp d tan(0.53 deg) wide -- 9.3 mm per metre of standoff, held to 10 % -- where a binary test gives a step of zero width and fails outright.

docs/physics-model.md §5.2, §6.1; ADR 0095, ADR 0107; roadmap PT.22.
