Rotor isaac

Rotor discs on a stage (ADR 0081): prim transform -> veil, and the occlusion that keeps a blade off the motor bell it is bolted to.

tests/unit/test_rotor_isaac.py on GitHub

Engine-free despite living in irsim_isaac: nothing here imports omni or pxr, exactly as motion_isaac takes its stage as an argument. The test that earns its keep is the last one -- occlusion by the disc plane rather than by the disc centre's range, which for a 75-degree tilt at 20 m is a difference of two thirds of a metre across the ellipse.
