Temperature encoding

Non-negotiable #2 at the encode/decode boundary (docs/physics-model.md §13.3, §15 Tier 1).

tests/unit/test_temperature_encoding.py on GitHub

The positive test alone proves nothing -- a round trip through any dtype "passes" if the tolerance is loose enough. The negative controls establish that the same measurement detects the failure mode it exists for: raw kelvin, or even encoded c, forced through float16 lands far outside 10 mK. If a negative control ever passes the 10 mK bound, the round-trip test has lost its sensitivity and must be redesigned before anything else is touched.
