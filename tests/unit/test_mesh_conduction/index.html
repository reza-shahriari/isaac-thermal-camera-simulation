Mesh conduction

Lateral conduction between a mesh's cells (WM.6).

tests/unit/test_mesh_conduction.py on GitHub

Two things can go wrong here and neither shows up as an error. A conductance of the wrong magnitude renders a gradient that is simply too strong or too weak, and nothing complains; a negative conductance breaks the discrete maximum principle and puts a cell outside the range its neighbours and its forcing span, which arrives as a bright speck indistinguishable from a bad pixel. The tests below measure the first against the fin equation's own answer and pin the second shut.
