Fpa thermal

FPA temperature node and gain/offset(T_FPA) polynomials (M9.2, ADR 0053): the steady state, the time constant recovered from the 63.2 % crossing, normalisation at T_cal, the uniform DN shift a 5 K ambient rise produces (and its absence when TEC-pinned), per-step energy conservation, and the one-weather-object guard (CLAUDE.md #6).
