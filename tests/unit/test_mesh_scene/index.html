Mesh scene

WM.7 — a mesh temperature field, reachable from a scene config.

tests/unit/test_mesh_scene.py on GitHub

WM.2 built the field and WM.3 put it on pixels, and until this row nothing could ask for one: no scene config could declare a mesh, so every shipped scene rendered its curved prims at a single temperature and the whole lane was a capability with no user.

configs/scenes/quad_flight_mesh.yaml is the first scene that uses it. It is the aerial mission of quad_flight_pointwise.yaml with the two arms as tubes instead of flat strips, which is what an arm actually is — and the difference is the ~27 K between a sunlit carbon crown and an underside sitting on air temperature, a spread a single patch normal cannot represent.

docs/physics-model.md §6.1, §13.1; ADR 0110 (the field on the mesh), ADR 0087, ADR 0109; roadmap WM.7.
