Defocus otf

OC.2 -- the defocus OTF models (docs/physics-model.md §8.3).

tests/unit/test_defocus_otf.py on GitHub

The load-bearing test is the first one: Hopkins reduces to the diffraction MTF at zero defocus. That is what licenses it to replace the cascade's first factor rather than multiply onto it, and it is a property no amount of plausible-looking blur would give you by accident.
