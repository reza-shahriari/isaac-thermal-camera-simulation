Housing field

SC.17: the housing is seen through the field, not added as one number (§8.2, ADR 0145).

tests/unit/test_housing_field.py on GitHub

A pixel at field angle θ sees the aperture as the projected solid angle Ω_eff RI_ij and the inside of the camera over the rest of its hemisphere. With lens and housing at one temperature the power on a uniform scene, referenced to the optical axis, is

Φ_ij = A_d Ω_eff [L_h + τ_opt RI_ij (L_scene − L_h)]

so the relative illumination multiplies the scene minus the housing. Every test here would fail on the pre-SC.17 form Φ = A_d Ω_eff [τ RI L_scene + (1 − τ) L_h], which darkens the corners of every scene, including one at the housing temperature.
