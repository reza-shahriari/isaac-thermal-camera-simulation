Boson sitf golden

Golden SITF of the committed Boson configuration (M4.8): noiseless floor DN for ten extended blackbodies through the calibrated bolometer transfer, keyed on the config hash, 1 DN tolerance. A regenerated response file or a band-block change reports STALE; a change in the transfer, the LUT or the optics with the same inputs reports FAILING.
