Noise cube golden

Golden noise cube (M4.10): 8 frames of 64x64 pre-quantisation signal from the Boson detector + NoiseStage on a uniform 300 K flux, keyed on the config hash *and* the NumPy version (the Box-Muller log/cos can move by an ulp between libms, so a NumPy change is reported STALE rather than FAILING). Compared at 1e-4 DN, about 1e-6 of the noise sigma.
